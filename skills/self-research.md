# self-research

Return evidence that explains which methods fit the workspace's requirement and why.
Use shared research articles and reuse adequate answers in place.
Leave proposals, implementation and competency completion to their owners.

## Frame the choice

Use the caller's assignment and any supplied competency or workspace premise.
State the unresolved choice and what an adequate answer must establish.
A direct brief needs no fabricated competency.

Study the relevant discipline's methods.
Keep candidate mechanisms open to comparison.
Use [[a-kind-or-a-question::au-agent-guides]] when existing authoring guidance may settle the choice.
Research is useful when the missing method could change the design.

Honor the caller's scope and budget.
Return new requirements through [[derive]].
Keep private repository details out of external queries.

## Reuse or investigate

Use [Read research evidence](#read-research-evidence) before starting new work.
An adequate answer needs no replacement article or question.
Reuse current reads until relevant state changes or freshness is uncertain.

Choose a research field by its premise's intent and exclusions.
A field can serve several competencies.
Reuse a suitable field and recover unfinished preparation before creating another.
Carry known scope forward and ask only about unresolved boundaries that affect the investigation.

Use au-tree-research for the work it owns:

| Need | Skill |
| --- | --- |
| Establish a field | [[research-premise::au-tree-research]] |
| Develop missing questions | [[mediate::au-tree-research]] |
| Answer a question or recover research | [[research::au-tree-research]] |
| Update the field's map | [[refresh-map::au-tree-research]] |

Read the selected skills and their current contracts before dependent work.
Mediation invokes refresh-map, which requires refresh_research_map.
These skills are guidance, not callable tools.
Report missing delivery and distinguish following source instructions from execution through a selected skill.

Supply the question, available evidence and stopping condition.
Carry the caller's paths and writing guidance.
Use the caller's profile or the active profile.
With neither, use [[core-profile::au-writing-style]].

When a competency is supplied, preserve its existing research selection and add relevant whole-file questions where possible.
The competency selects questions but is not their tree parent.
If selection cannot be saved, keep the evidence and limitation on the caller's existing work.
Preparation alone does not answer the research assignment.

## Require a useful methodological answer

Give the shared research skill the criteria the design choice needs:
- Explain mechanisms through concrete cases
- Identify assumptions, costs and limitations
- Compare credible alternatives and failures that would change the recommendation
- Explain which methods combine and which conflict
- Address other conditions that determine suitability

Ground decisive claims in primary evidence.
Distinguish a documented mechanism from evidence of its effectiveness.
A catalogue of approaches does not settle the choice.
Supported individual claims may still leave the comparison unanswered.

Keep comparisons together when they need one explanation.
Use [[keep-a-note-whole::au-agent-guides]] for that judgment.
Let the difficulty of the choice determine depth.
Shared sources do not excuse shallow answers to distinct questions.

## Judge sufficiency and return

Read the answers together against the assignment.
Can a reader apply the methods to a concrete case and explain when to choose differently?
Name missing evidence that could change the decision.

Use research's recovery rules for incomplete answers.
Use mediation when a completed answer leaves a distinct methodological gap.
Do not reopen completed research or create an additional answer without the authorization its contract requires.
Leave other open questions in place when this competency has enough evidence.

Continue useful investigation within the assigned scope and budget.
Otherwise return the supported synthesis, sources and remaining limitation.
Keep the next action on the caller's existing work.
Leave competency states unchanged.

Write through the engine.
Read changed records for meaning and inspect write diagnostics.
Repair introduced errors and assess relevant warnings before relying on the result.
Research completion alone does not establish design sufficiency.

## Read research evidence

This reading serves self-research and propose.
It creates no questions and changes no research states.

**Select the evidence**

Read a supplied competency's own research values.
Selection includes named questions, not their descendants.
Also consider caller-supplied evidence outside that selection.
Find articles through each article's own answers relation.
Several articles can answer one question.

Use qualified family queries with origins: ["file"] when membership is unknown.
Page completely before claiming coverage or absence.
Backlinks narrow candidates but do not establish a relation.

**Use the engine's results**

Read effective top-level values, including body contributions.
Exclude nested look-alikes and prose mentions.
Use [[qualify-colliding-fields::au-agent-guides]] when origins collide.
If the view omits an authored value, read the qualified frontmatter and combine only same-origin contributions.

Reuse resolved identities and diagnostics.
Inspect authored references when normalization can hide an invalid commit pin.
Research selections and article answers require live references.
Resolve missing identity or fragment details through the engine.
Reject double-caret block referents before invoking whole-file research.
Headings and single-caret navigation identify the containing file.
Treat unreadable relations as incomplete evidence, not empty selections.

**Judge support**

Read relevant articles and supplied evidence in full.
Use [[research::au-tree-research]] and [[article::au-tree-research]] for article requirements and completion checks.
Judge relevance and support beyond structural validity.

Reuse adequate evidence without copying it.
When a revision matters, record its repository, path and full commit separately from live references.
Check retrieval before claiming recoverability.
Leave unfinished answers pending and retain dropped leads as context.
