# propose

Return one supported change the human can assess.
An adequate existing route or a precise unresolved dependency can be the result.
Keep decisions and evidence on the proposal or the caller's existing work.

## Establish the case

Use the caller's brief or competency to establish the requirement.
Read the evidence and relevant current capabilities.
Reuse current reads until relevant state changes or freshness is uncertain.

[[improve]] assesses recurring friction.
Check the supplied grounding when it leaves a consequential uncertainty.
For shared research, use [[self-research#Read research evidence]].
That reading does not start research or require a new competency.

Compare the proposed change with adequate existing routes.
Choose the simplest design that meets the requirement.
A supported local remedy needs no invented research.

## Ground the design

Read the owning guides and contracts where the choice depends on them.
Inspect relevant producer and consumer implementations when declared structure does not establish the behavior.
Resolve a consequential disagreement against current implementation and a focused check.

Use guidance for the decision at hand:
- [[compose-a-capability::au-agent-guides]] for cooperating mechanisms
- [[pick-the-vehicle::au-agent-guides]] for delivery
- [[the-capability-pattern::au-agent-guides]] for discovery
- [[design-for-growth::au-agent-guides]] for contract changes
- Other owning guidance for unresolved choices

Explain how the intended consumer reaches and uses the result.
Account for permitted effects and any migration, activation or recovery the change needs.
A field grants no operation new effects.
An audit needs a doctrine and a caller that runs it.
Its scope does not schedule execution.

Keep a small design in the proposal.
Use [[design-architecture]] when substantial interactions need their own explanation.
Identify the design revision that determines the proposed scope.

## Limit the commitment to its support

For a consequential methodological choice, compare alternatives and the conditions that change the recommendation.
Supported individual claims may still leave that comparison unanswered.

Return a missing method to [[self-research]] with:
- The unresolved question and blocked decision
- Evidence already read
- What an adequate answer must establish

Continue independently supported work.
A prerequisite may be useful while leaving the larger competency open.
Use answers only for requirements the completed scope promises to fulfill.
Preserve obligations outside that scope.

Check the uncertain behavior with a case that could distinguish the alternatives.
Separate expected acceptance results from observations.
Do not promise behavior whose basis remains unresolved.

## Save and return

Reuse an existing proposal for the same scope.
Preserve rejected decisions and explain changed grounds before proposing that scope again.

Use the current proposal.proposed contract.
Put actual supporting evidence in grounds.
Selected research questions alone are not evidence.
Keep live references in the field.
When a revision matters, record its repository, path and full commit in the explanation.
Check retrieval before claiming recoverability and preserve reviewed uncommitted content.

Write through the engine.
Read the saved proposal for meaning and inspect write diagnostics.
Repair introduced errors and assess relevant warnings before relying on it.

Return the proposal, existing route or dependency with the next action.
[[improve]] owns approved implementation and competency closure.
A design revision does not expand approval.
